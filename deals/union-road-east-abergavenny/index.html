3 Bedroom Terraced House, Union Road East, Abergavenny, NP7

Union Road East, Abergavenny, NP7

Description

Chain Free | Character Three Bedroom Home | Prime Abergavenny Location | Ideal for Homeowners & Investors

Offered to the market with no onward chain, this charming three-bedroom terraced home combines character, generous living space and an excellent location, making it an attractive opportunity for homeowners and investors alike.

Situated on the ever-popular Union Road East, the property is just a short stroll from Abergavenny town centre, renowned for its independent cafes, restaurants, boutique shops and thriving community. Surrounded by the Brecon Beacons National Park, Abergavenny offers the perfect balance of vibrant market town living and beautiful countryside, making it one of South Wales' most desirable places to call home or place to visit.

Inside, the property is full of warmth and character, featuring a welcoming entrance hall, a bright bay-fronted lounge flooded with natural light, a spacious separate dining room ideal for entertaining or family living, a well-appointed kitchen with ample storage, and a convenient ground floor shower room. Upstairs offers three well-proportioned bedrooms, including two generous doubles, together with a separate WC.

Externally, the attractive traditional frontage and front garden create excellent kerb appeal while complementing the character of this home.

For investors, the location is particularly attractive for high rental demand, with Nevill Hall Hospital within walking distance, excellent transport links and consistently strong demand from professionals, hospital staff and families as well as opportunity for serviced accommodation (subject to own due diligence). For owner occupiers, this is a fantastic opportunity to enjoy a spacious home in a sought-after location with everything Abergavenny has to offer right on your doorstep.

Properties in this location are always in demand, so early enquiry is highly recommended.
