Why VM

Why work with VM.

Property sourcing done properly means being honest about numbers, clear about risk, and consistent in how you operate. That is what we aim for on every deal.

I

Rigorous deal analysis

Every deal is modelled across multiple investment strategies with full financial analysis — cash flow, yield, ROI, break-even occupancy and exit scenarios. You get a clear picture of the opportunity, including where the risks are.

II

Off-market only

We do not recycle open market listings. Every deal is sourced directly from motivated vendors and landlords before it reaches Rightmove — giving investors access to opportunities that are not available elsewhere.

III

Straightforward with vendors

We build relationships with sellers and landlords by being honest about what we can do and following through on what we say. That consistency is what generates repeat referrals and reliable deal flow.

IV

Fully compliant operation

VM Property Sourcing Ltd is registered with Companies House, the ICO, HMRC for AML Supervision and The Property Ombudsman redress scheme. We operate within the regulatory framework and put everything in writing.

Co-Sourcing

Co-Sourcing Partnerships.

We work with other compliant property sourcers on a co-sourcing basis. Whether you have deals but need investors, or investors but need deals — we are open to a conversation. We operate under written agreements, split fees transparently and work only with compliant partners.

You source, we connect

You have off-market deals but need access to investors. We will present your deals to our network under a written co-sourcing agreement with an agreed, transparent fee split.

We source, you connect

You have an investor network but need consistent, well-packaged deal flow. We will source, analyse and present deals for your buyers on agreed terms — compliantly and professionally.

Referral arrangements

If you know a motivated vendor or landlord but do not operate in that area or strategy, we are happy to discuss a referral arrangement. Compensation is agreed in writing before any introduction is made.
